Terms & Conditions
1. Payment
Full payment is due prior to or upon delivery. Renter authorizes Mo's Hauling, LLC to charge any additional fees including overweight charges, extended rental fees, contamination fees, trip fees, and damage costs.
2. Overweight / Overage
Base price includes up to 2 tons (4,000 lbs). Excess weight is billed at $95 per ton. Renter is responsible for all excess weight charges. Overloaded containers will not be picked up until resolved.
3. Prohibited Items
The following are prohibited and will result in additional fees or refusal of service:
  • Hazardous materials (paints, solvents, chemicals, asbestos)
  • Tires, batteries, appliances with refrigerants
  • Electronics (unless pre-approved)
  • Liquids, biohazards, or medical waste
4. Placement & Property Responsibility
Renter is responsible for designating a safe, accessible placement location on private property. Renter assumes all liability for any damage to driveways, landscaping, or other surfaces. Mo's Hauling is not responsible for driveway damage.
5. Container Use
Do not overfill the container above the rim. All debris must be level with or below the top edge. Materials extending above the sides are not permitted and may result in refusal of pickup or additional charges.
6. Right to Refuse Service
Company reserves the right to refuse delivery or pickup at its sole discretion. If service cannot be completed due to prohibited items or unsafe conditions, trip fees and downtime charges may apply.
7. Rental Period & Extensions
Rental period begins on the delivery date. Extensions beyond the agreed period must be requested in advance. Additional daily or weekly rates apply for extensions and will be charged to the payment method on file.
8. Early Pickup
Early pickup requests are subject to availability. No refunds are provided for unused rental days. Minimum rental fee applies regardless of duration.
9. Cancellations
Cancellations made less than 24 hours before scheduled delivery may incur a trip fee. No-shows or inaccessible delivery locations on the scheduled day will be charged a full trip fee.
10. Permits
Renter is solely responsible for obtaining any required municipal or HOA permits for container placement on public property or right-of-way. Mo's Hauling assumes no liability for permit violations.
11. Indemnification
Renter agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Mo's Hauling, LLC and its employees from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from the renter's use of the container or services.
12. Limitation of Liability
To the fullest extent permitted by law, Company's total liability shall not exceed the rental fee paid. Company is not liable for incidental, consequential, or indirect damages.
