Information we collect

What you give us

When you fill out a quote or booking form, call, text, or email us, you may give us:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • The address where we'll deliver a dumpster or do the removal
  • The service you want, your preferred date and time, and a description of the job
  • Photos of the items or area (optional)
  • Your drawn signature, if you sign a dumpster rental agreement with us

What we collect automatically

When you visit our site, we and our analytics and advertising providers receive basic technical information: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the page or ad that sent you to us, and your approximate location based on your IP address. This is collected with cookies, pixels, and similar technology (see Cookies & advertising).

What comes from scheduling

If you book through our scheduling page, Calendly collects the details you enter there and passes them to us. Calendly's own privacy policy governs what it does with that information.

How we use it

  • Reply to your request, give you a quote, and schedule the job
  • Deliver and pick up dumpsters, haul away your items, and complete the work
  • Confirm appointments and contact you about your job by phone, text, or email
  • Prepare rental agreements and invoices, and keep the business records we're required to keep
  • Plan routes and locate service addresses on a map in our internal scheduling tools
  • Understand how our website performs and measure and improve our advertising
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with the law

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not share mobile phone numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Cookies & advertising

Our site uses cookies and similar technology from Google and Meta to count visits, measure how our ads perform, and show our ads to people who have visited us. Those companies may combine this information with other information they hold, under their own privacy policies. Some state privacy laws describe this kind of activity as "targeted advertising" or "sharing."

You can limit it at any time:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings
  • Adjust Google's ad settings and Meta's ad preferences
  • Use the industry opt-out tools at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org

Photos you send us

Photos are used to prepare your quote and to plan the job. Please leave out people's faces, ID documents, and anything else sensitive. Once your job is done, we treat them like the rest of your job records (see How long we keep it).

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it to quote, perform, and support your service; to keep business, tax, and insurance records; to resolve disputes; and to follow the law. After that, we delete it or remove the details that identify you.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information — including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access-controlled business tools, and established service providers. No method of sending or storing data is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute security. Please don't send payment card numbers or government ID numbers through our forms.

Your choices & rights

Residents of Colorado and some other states have legal rights over their personal information. We're happy to extend these to every customer:

  • Access a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information, subject to records we're required to keep
  • Opt out of targeted advertising and sharing (see Cookies & advertising)

To make a request, email junk@moshauling.co or call (720) 340-8144. We may need to confirm it's really you before we act on it, and we'll respond within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can reply to appeal; if you're still not satisfied, Colorado residents may contact the Colorado Attorney General's office. We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. For text messages, reply STOP.

Children

Our website and services are meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business or the law changes. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll say so on our website.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us any day of the week.
